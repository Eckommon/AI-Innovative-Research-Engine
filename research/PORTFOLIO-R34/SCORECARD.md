## 1. US-EPA-XMEDIA-001 — **41/45**

### Mission bottleneck — 5/5

Cross-program environmental compliance risk is a genuine facility-level operational / regulatory bottleneck. A facility can be structurally connected across hazardous-waste and water-discharge programs while compliance history is usually analyzed within program silos.

### Cross-source value — 5/5

The candidate requires a meaningful relationship across **RCRAInfo / ECHO RCRA**, **FRS**, and **ICIS-NPDES** rather than a same-table correlation. FRS is explicitly the official cross-program identity bridge.

### Direct outcome — 5/5

Future NPDES effluent-limit exceedance is a direct regulatory monitoring outcome represented in ICIS-NPDES, not an indirect corporate score or generic enforcement proxy.

### Independent-unit prospect — 4/5

EPA's national facility systems are large and heterogeneous, but the exact fail-closed RCRA↔FRS↔NPDES overlap and longitudinal support have not yet been measured prospectively. Full credit is withheld until F01 establishes the actual exact cross-program universe rather than inferring it from source size.

### Practical value — 5/5

If a later relationship survives preregistered testing, it could inform cross-program inspection triage, compliance-monitoring allocation, facility risk screening and environmental due diligence. This does not imply a causal or enforcement recommendation at R34.

### Zero-cost operability — 4/5

Current official EPA downloads and web services are public and zero-cost. Full national NPDES effluent-violation data are large, so a future gate must use official jurisdictional or bounded web-service routes where appropriate rather than assume inexpensive full-national loading.

### Join defensibility — 5/5

The official FRS Registry ID / program-linkage architecture provides the strongest possible class of cross-program identity available for this mission. No name/address/geospatial/manual repair is needed or authorized.

### Next-gate information gain — 5/5

An outcome-blind F01 can cheaply answer several unresolved high-value questions at once: exact cross-program overlap, date support, facility/permit multiplicity, exact identity consistency, zero-cost endpoint executability and descendant source-cardinality feasibility.

### Low overlap / novelty risk — 3/5

Credit is deliberately capped. EPA already conducts multi-media / cross-statute compliance screening, and the repository has a prior RCRA branch. However, the prior internal relationship was FEMA disaster → RCRA CEI `FOUND_VIOLATION`, not historical RCRA compliance → future NPDES effluent exceedance. The bounded scan found substantial adjacent work but no materially near-identical frozen prospective design. **This is not a novelty claim.**

**Frozen total: 41/45.**

## 4. US-BTS-PORT-001 — **30/45**

### Mission bottleneck — 5/5

Port congestion and throughput are major supply-chain bottlenecks.

### Cross-source value — 5/5

BTS/USCG-AIS operational port measures combined with Census trade flows are genuinely cross-source and economically meaningful.

### Direct outcome — 5/5

Port-level trade throughput / shipping weight is direct rather than a proxy.

### Independent-unit prospect — 2/5

The published port universe is relatively small, and the FTA N01 lesson requires discounting designs that may collapse into few independent units / strata even when time-series rows are numerous.

### Practical value — 5/5

The relationship could be useful for supply-chain disruption monitoring.

### Zero-cost operability — 3/5

Official current weekly berthing statistics exist, but bounded revalidation did not establish a stable current public machine-readable per-port endpoint equivalent to the visible dashboard, and dashboard access itself is not an authorized scraping route.

### Join defensibility — 1/5

No exact official BTS port identity → Census Schedule-D crosswalk was established. BTS explicitly warns that port boundaries can differ across source systems. Name/geospatial/manual repair is prohibited.

### Next-gate information gain — 3/5

An F01 could determine whether the newer weekly product repaired the old identity blocker, but much of the gate would revisit a problem already observed in `US-PORT-F01`.

### Low overlap / novelty risk — 1/5

AIS-based port stay / congestion / throughput estimation is already a mature topic. Some implementation value may remain, but low-overlap credit is minimal.

**Frozen total: 30/45.**
