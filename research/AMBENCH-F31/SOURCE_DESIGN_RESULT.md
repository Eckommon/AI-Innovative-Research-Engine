  - L67: Each measurement includes 3 repeats. The mean values and standard deviations are tabulated. For the final melt pool track image segmentation, the three repeats were aligned and saved as a single TIFF file with values of 255 where all three melt pools agree, values of 170 where only two melt pools agree, values of 85 where one melt pool exists, and values of 0 outside the melt pools. A simple uncertainty analysis was included for the measurands listed above, which includes four components of uncertainty that were combined using root mean square method and expanded using a coverage factor of k = 2 to establish p = 95 % confidence bounds. The four components are the standard error of the mean based on three repeats (Type A), resolution (Type B), user selection (Type B), and variability along the track length (Type B). The individual components, combined, and expanded values are included in tables.
