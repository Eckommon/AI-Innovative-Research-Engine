## Official-source revalidation

### 1. US-PHMSA-PIPE-001

PHMSA official source surfaces establish:
- source-native operator identification numbers (OpIDs);
- annual-report data for gas distribution, gas gathering, gas transmission, hazardous liquids/CO2, LNG and underground natural gas storage;
- historical/superseded report forms and instructions back to 2010;
- annual reports with infrastructure variables including mileage, facilities, commodities, material and installation dates;
- incident/accident source files downloadable free of charge.

Strengths:
- exact operator-level identity prospect;
- clearly separated annual infrastructure and incident source families;
- long explicit historical lineage rather than replace-in-place cadence;
- broad operator-level structural variation;
- direct operational-safety event.

Critical boundaries:
- F01 must freeze exactly one pipeline facility family before empirical row access;
- operator reorganizations/OpID changes must be handled source-natively;
- prior incident/enforcement/inspection history cannot become predictive exposure;
- serious/significant event definitions must remain source-native and prospectively frozen;
- earlier PHMSA highway-hazmat export access limitation remains a transport-risk signal, though it was a different data family.

### 2. US-FAA-AIR-001

FAA Releasable Aircraft Database currently:
- is refreshed daily;
- includes Registration Master, Document Index, reference data and a Deregistered Aircraft file;
- exposes downloadable yearly registration database archives for 2012–2021.

Strengths:
- explicit historical archived bodies;
- direct deregistered-aircraft event source;
- physically meaningful asset lifecycle;
- substantial structural covariate universe.

Critical boundaries:
- N-number alone is reassignable;
- F01 must establish a stable exact aircraft identity from documented source fields without owner-name/address repair;
- deregistration reason is heterogeneous and may include export, owner request, destruction, retirement or other administrative causes;
- current post-2021 daily states are not automatically distinct archived bodies.

### 3. US-CMS-REV-001

CMS official data dictionary establishes:
- `ENRLMT_ID` as a unique 15-character enrollment identifier;
- NPI as a separate provider identifier;
- Revocation Reason;
- Revocation Effective Date;
- Re-enrollment Bar Expiration Date.

Strengths:
- excellent enrollment-level identity;
- direct regulator-defined revocation event/date;
- high practical regulatory value;
- enrollment structure can be separated from the later revocation file.

Critical boundaries:
- the revoked dataset is a current-under-re-enrollment-bar population, not a proven complete historical revocation archive;
- multiple enrollments and multiple NPIs require strict enrollment-level handling;
- current approved-enrollment baseline creates survivor/common-support questions;
- enforcement/compliance precursors must be excluded.

### 4. US-NCUA-CU-001

NCUA official surfaces establish:
- quarterly Call Report data from March 1994 onward;
- downloadable quarter-specific ZIP files;
- active federally insured credit-union listings;
- dated conservatorship and liquidation history;
- source semantics distinguishing conservatorship, merger and involuntary liquidation/closure.

Strengths:
- exceptionally strong quarterly historical lineage;
- rich financial/operational structure;
- direct institutional end-state;
- high practical financial-stability value.

Critical boundary:
- the public liquidation/conservatorship event surface reviewed here exposes name/city/state/type/status but does not directly demonstrate the same charter identifier used in quarterly Call Reports.
- If exact event-to-call-report identity is not exposed in an official source, F01 must HOLD rather than use name/location reconciliation.
