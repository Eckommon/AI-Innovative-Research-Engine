### 1. `CA-CRA-CHARITY-001` — charity structure → subsequent registration revocation

Unit: one Canadian registered charity / qualified donee keyed by source-native CRA registration/business number.

- historical exposure prospect: annual T3010 structure, designation/category, financial/activity/program characteristics;
- future event prospect: later CRA registration revocation under source-native effective-status/revocation semantics;
- official source strength: Government of Canada Open Data publishes annual List of Charities CSV resources across decades; CRA separately publishes revocation lists and publicly available status data;
- exact identity prospect: Business Number / Registration Number;
- anti-tautology: filing delinquency, direct revocation-warning/status fields and failure-to-file precursor may not become predictive exposure;
- F01: prove annual identity continuity, revocation identity/date semantics, reinstatement handling, support and future-membership seal.

### 2. `UK-CC-CHARITY-001` — registered-charity structure → subsequent removal from register

Unit: one England/Wales charity keyed by Charity Commission charity number.

- historical exposure prospect: financial history, classification, trustee/employee and organisational structure from official register/API/bulk extracts;
- future event prospect: later removal from the Register of Charities;
- official source strength: Charity Commission provides full-register bulk JSON/TXT/API; data definitions include `date_of_removal`, `removal_reason` and reporting status;
- exact identity: charity number;
- limitation: current/live register representation versus durable historical snapshot lineage must be proven;
- anti-tautology: overdue/default reporting status or direct removal precursor may not become predictive exposure.

### 3. `AU-ACNC-CHARITY-001` — charity structure → subsequent loss of current registration

### 4. `US-FDIC-BANK-001` — bank structure → subsequent FDIC-insured bank failure

Unit: one FDIC-insured institution keyed by exact FDIC CERT.

- future event prospect: later source-native bank failure;
- official source strength: FDIC provides current/historical data back to 1992, Call Report/bulk financial data, institution/history data and failures/assistance data;
- exact identity: FDIC certificate number;
- limitation: bank-failure prediction is a highly mature research domain and events are rare;
- anti-tautology: direct supervisory/enforcement/failure-warning variables prohibited.
