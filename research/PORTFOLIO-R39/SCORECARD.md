- two independently published IRS public-disclosure families with exact EIN identity;
- a direct later legal-status event with explicit effective/posting/reinstatement dates;
- very large organization-level support;
- anonymous zero-cost bulk access;
- a cheap outcome-blind F01 capable of falsifying historical XML/index continuity, EIN coverage, event-schema semantics and future-period sealing before any revocation membership is opened.

The score deliberately grants only **2/5** on low-overlap/novelty. Form-990 financial vulnerability and nonprofit failure are established research topics. The selected branch therefore may not claim generic financial weakness → nonprofit failure as new. Its only authorized next step is a structural F01 for the narrower source package and anti-tautological prospective design.
