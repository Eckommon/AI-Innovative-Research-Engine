# US-RCRA-N01 — Bounded Literature Adjudication

## Current evidence boundary

A targeted current search was performed before any paired `FOUND_VIOLATION` result was opened.

Material adjacent prior work exists and must constrain claims:

- EPA RCRA compliance-monitoring guidance identifies the Compliance Evaluation Inspection (CEI) as the primary compliance-monitoring mechanism and standard comprehensive inspection for operating TSDFs. This establishes that inspection opportunity is part of the observed compliance process, not a nuisance that can be ignored.
  - https://www.epa.gov/compliance/compliance-monitoring-strategy-resource-conservation-and-recovery-act
  - https://www.epa.gov/compliance/hazardous-waste-compliance-monitoring
- Stafford (2002), *The Effect of Punishment on Firm Compliance with Hazardous Waste Regulations*, explicitly models inspection levels because detected RCRA violations and inspection likelihood vary together. This is material prior evidence for surveillance/detection bias.
  - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0095069601912045
- GAO-25-106253 evaluates natural-hazard/climate-risk management at hazardous-waste TSDFs and discusses inspections, permits and contingency planning. Therefore static natural-hazard vulnerability/exposure of RCRA facilities is not a novel contribution.
  - https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-25-106253/index.html
- Current RCRA predictive-compliance work and operational research use inspection/violation history for targeting, further limiting any generic claim that RCRA compliance prediction itself is novel.

## Bounded adjudication

The current targeted search did **not** surface a materially near-identical national design that:

1. restricts to operating TSDFs;
2. assigns exact FEMA major-disaster county exposure by incident-begin date;
3. selects one prospectively defined index disaster under washout/multi-disaster rules;
4. pairs the same facility's last pre-disaster CEI with its first post-disaster CEI under fixed windows; and
5. tests the paired CEI compliance result only after structural support is preregistered.

Therefore the N01 novelty-overlap gate is provisionally **adjacent, not near-identical**.

This is **not proof of novelty**. Any later paper/product claim must describe the contribution narrowly as a preregistered paired-CEI disaster/compliance monitoring design, not as novel RCRA hazard mapping, novel RCRA compliance prediction, or a causal natural-disaster effect.
