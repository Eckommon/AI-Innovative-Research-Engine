# PORTFOLIO-R44 — bounded source / overlap revalidation

## 1. EU-EMA-MA-001

EMA currently publishes downloadable medicine data tables that are automatically updated overnight. Medicine/EPAR pages expose exact EMA product number, marketing-authorisation issue date and, where applicable, withdrawal date. EMA also publicly explains that withdrawn/expired central authorisations are published with reasons.

Structural strengths:
- exact source-native EMA product number;
- direct authorisation/withdrawal dates;
- official withdrawal reason available on individual EPAR pages;
- public downloadable tables and reproducible official web surfaces.

Primary unresolved risk:
- event heterogeneity. Voluntary/commercial withdrawal, regulatory/safety withdrawal, suspension, expiry and application withdrawal may not be pooled.
- F01 must prove a deterministic current source path for product identity plus reason/date semantics without selecting a medicine because its outcome is known.

Overlap:
- pharmaceutical/regulatory withdrawal is a mature area. Novelty credit is conservative; value lies in the exact public-data prospective structural design.

## 2. US-FERC-HYDRO-001

FERC currently publishes a public Active Licenses hydropower dataset and supports direct CSV/XLSX download. Exact FERC Project Number is a strong identity prospect.

Primary unresolved risk:
- the current source surface clearly establishes active licences, but the zero-cost machine-readable surrender/termination event family and historical lineage are not yet established.
- F01 would have high information gain but a non-trivial source risk.

Overlap:
- infrastructure/regulatory-state themes overlap prior utility/EIA/FAA work, though the project-level licence identity and event family are distinct.

## 3. AU-ACNC-CHARITY-001

ACNC officially publishes downloadable Charity Register and annual Annual Information Statement datasets. Exact ABN is a strong cross-dataset identity prospect.

Primary unresolved risk:
- revocation reason/date history and prospective future membership must be separated from filing-default triggers.
- filing default/nonlodgment or pending-revocation status would be mechanically proximate and is prohibited as exposure.

Overlap:
- severe conceptual overlap with prior IRS exempt-organisation and UK-charity branches. This materially reduces marginal information value and novelty credit.

## 4. UK-IPO-TM-001

Official UKIPO Trade Mark Journal pages expose exact UK application numbers and publication dates.

Primary unresolved risk:
- anonymous zero-cost bulk historical/current status lineage has not been established from the official source surfaces reviewed here.
- ordinary expiry/non-renewal is mechanically time-driven and cannot substitute for a prospectively defined adverse status such as refusal/revocation/invalidation.

Overlap:
- IP-maintenance/status analysis is mature and operational source risk remains higher than the other candidates.
