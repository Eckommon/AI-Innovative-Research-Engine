# DEC-124 — Preregister US-AIR-E01 and require weather-quality Stage A
# DEC-124 — US-AIR-E01 사전등록 및 기상 품질 Stage A 의무화

## Decision / 결정

Authorize Issue #90 `US-AIR-E01` only under the preregistration in `research/US-AIR-E01/README.md`.

The single primary weather exposure is frozen as NOAA LCDv2 **`DailyPrecipitation`**, transformed as `log1p(mm)`. / 단일 primary 기상변수는 NOAA LCDv2 `DailyPrecipitation`, 변환은 `log1p(mm)`으로 고정한다.

## Stage ordering / 단계 순서

**Stage A must execute before any delay magnitude is parsed.**

Stage A may inspect only the frozen precipitation values/quality needed to determine prospective source support from the F01 deterministic mapping.

Stage B may open `DepDelayMinutes` only if Stage A durably passes:
- >=120 qualified origin AirportIDs;
- >=120 represented NOAA station IDs;
- each qualified airport has >=330 usable assigned precipitation days;
- each month has >=20 usable assigned days.

No predictor substitution is allowed after failure.

## Frozen relationship design / 고정 관계 설계

Primary unit: airport-day.  
Primary outcome: mean eligible `DepDelayMinutes`.  
Baseline: airport FE + calendar-date FE + `log1p(scheduled departures)`.  
Weather model adds `log1p(DailyPrecipitation_mm)`.  
Inference: two-way CR1 by AirportID and FlightDate.

Primary material relationship gate requires positive `beta`, two-sided 95% CI lower > 0, and a model-implied >=1.0 minute difference between 0 and 10 mm precipitation.

## Boundary / 경계

E01 is contemporaneous association only. It does not authorize causal, propagation, prediction, ranking, novelty or utility claims. / E01은 당일 연관성 검증이며 인과·전파·예측·순위·신규성·실용성 주장을 승인하지 않는다.

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**.
