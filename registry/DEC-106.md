## Scientific interpretation / 과학적 해석

The result supports a replicated structure in which several prospectively selected critical-mineral mappings exhibit material concentration of **published U.S. general-import trade value** on both foreign-source and domestic unlading-district axes.

It does not prove physical tonnage concentration, causal disruption risk, inventory inadequacy, actual transaction-level port routing, or policy/investment superiority.

The result also cautions against assuming that foreign-source concentration and domestic-node concentration necessarily move together across minerals: the frozen eight-mineral descriptive rank association is negative. This is hypothesis-generating only.
