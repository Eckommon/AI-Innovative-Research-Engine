# CLM-085 — Threshold sensitivity is directionally stable while part-level block trajectories reverse sign / threshold 민감도는 일관되나 part별 block trajectory는 반대 부호

**KO:** AMBENCH-E24의 t80/t100/t120 area fixed-effect beta는 각각 `0.016772`, `0.015305`, `0.017831`로 모두 양수이며 material sign disagreement가 없다. 반면 part별 block-level Spearman rho는 Part1 `-0.460255`, Part2 `-0.268917`, Part3 `-0.483333`, Part4 `-0.694567`로 모두 음수다. 이 둘은 같은 estimand가 아니며, sign reversal은 common block/layer structure와 part hierarchy를 제거하지 않은 naive pooled interpretation이 부적절함을 보여주는 구조적 진단이다.

**EN:** The AMBENCH-E24 t80/t100/t120 area fixed-effect betas are `0.016772`, `0.015305`, and `0.017831`, all positive with no material sign disagreement. By contrast, part-specific block-level Spearman rhos are negative for all four parts: Part1 `-0.460255`, Part2 `-0.268917`, Part3 `-0.483333`, Part4 `-0.694567`. These are different estimands; the sign reversal is a structural diagnostic showing that naive pooled interpretation without explicit block/layer and part hierarchy is inappropriate.
