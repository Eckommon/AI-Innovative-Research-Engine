# DEC-175 — Select US-BRIDGE-F01 after terminal US-RCRA-E01

Select **US-BRIDGE-F01** as PORTFOLIO-R26's next independent branch at **41/45**.

Rationale:
- exact national bridge/county identity is available from FHWA NBI;
- 2015–2025 can remain inside the legacy Coding Guide format;
- actual inspection dates provide a defensible time axis;
- independent FEMA county/time disaster identity is zero-cost;
- bridge condition identities are direct inspected outcomes for a later gated test;
- the immediate F01 has high information gain without opening condition values.

Do not interpret the selection as novelty validation. BTS already publishes static bridge-hazard exposure and prior NBI deterioration literature exists. Static exposure mapping and generic deterioration prediction are outside the novelty claim.

Do not rescue US-RCRA-E01 or open bridge condition-rating values during R26 selection.
