## Why / 이유

NESO exposes a directly observed operational bottleneck outcome — daily inertia, voltage and thermal constraint costs/volumes — and half-hourly demand, embedded wind/solar, Scottish transfer and interconnector flows through public CKAN Data API/CSV resources.

Thermal constraints are source-defined as actions needed when natural energy flow between regions exceeds the capacity of connecting circuits. This is more direct than proxy-only grid stress measures.

The next uncertainty is source/time aggregation semantics and reproducible alignment, not credential acquisition or large-scale computation.

## Exposure disclosure / 사전 노출 고지

During source qualification before F01 preregistration, the first five public records of the **2025-2026** Constraint Breakdown and first five records of the **2026** Historic Demand resource were opened to inspect live schema/access behavior.

Therefore those particular first-five numerical records are considered exposed and must not be represented as newly outcome-blind evidence in a later experiment.

To preserve a cleaner future experiment route, F01 should preferentially qualify the current **2026-2027 Constraint Breakdown** source for schema/date alignment without emitting numerical outcome values. Any later controlled experiment must separately disclose all prior exposure and freeze its evaluation window before numerical analysis.
