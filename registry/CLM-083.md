# CLM-083 — Registered melt-pool area has statistically detectable but non-material XCT contribution / 등록 melt-pool area의 XCT 기여는 검출되지만 비물질적

**KO:** AMBENCH-E24의 사전등록 primary model에서 `melt_pool_area_t100_mm2`의 standardized beta는 `0.015305`, predictor partial R²는 `0.019321`, block-preserving permutation p는 `0.007900`이었다. 사전등록 materiality threshold `partial_R2 >= 0.05`를 충족하지 못하므로 이 결과는 통계적으로 검출 가능하더라도 material process↔XCT association으로 승격하지 않는다.

**EN:** In the preregistered AMBENCH-E24 primary model, `melt_pool_area_t100_mm2` had standardized beta `0.015305`, predictor partial R² `0.019321`, and block-preserving permutation p `0.007900`. Because the preregistered materiality threshold `partial_R2 >= 0.05` was not met, statistical detectability is not promoted to a material process↔XCT association.
