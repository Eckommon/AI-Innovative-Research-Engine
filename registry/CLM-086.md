# CLM-086 — Registered X4 XCT aggregate variation is block-dominant
# CLM-086 — Registered X4 XCT aggregate variation은 block 지배적이다

**Claim / 주장:** Under the exact E24 representation and aggregation, the registered X4 `xct_voxel_mean5` outcome is overwhelmingly structured by fixed 25-layer block/build progression. D25 reproduced E24 and found block-only R² `0.998820`, part+block R² `0.999421`, and block|part partial R² `0.999421`.

**Boundary / 경계:** This is a diagnostic statement about the E24 25-layer aggregate representation, not a causal statement about physical build progression. Part|block partial R² `0.509735` refers to the tiny residual left after block removes nearly all total variation and must not be interpreted as 51% of total outcome variance.
