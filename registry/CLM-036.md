# CLM-036 — BP4 Confocal Measurement Exists in Official Design Record / 공식 설계기록상 BP4 confocal 측정

**KO:** NIST AMB2022-03 공식 measurement/challenge 문서는 `AMB2022-718-SH1-BP4`를 3×7 single-track in-situ dynamic-laser-coupling plate로 식별하고, laser-coupling specimen이 ex-situ laser scanning confocal microscopy로 측정됐다고 명시한다. 또한 complete 3D surface profile에서 steady-state height profile, track-end mass accumulation/loss, chevron-feature shape 등의 정보를 추출했다고 설명한다.

**EN:** The official NIST AMB2022-03 measurement/challenge document identifies `AMB2022-718-SH1-BP4` as the 3×7 single-track in-situ dynamic-laser-coupling plate and states that the laser-coupling specimens were measured ex situ using laser scanning confocal microscopy. It further describes complete 3D surface profiles used to extract steady-state height profiles, track-end mass accumulation/loss, chevron-feature shapes, and related topography information.

**Evidence:** NIST `AMB2022-03 Benchmark Measurements and Challenge Problems`, Version 1.01, Sections 2.4/2.5/3.3.3; `research/AMBENCH-POST-E09-TRIAGE.md`.
