- source/download/integrity failure -> `HOLD_E43_RUNTIME_OR_INTEGRITY`;

- output/schema/<100 positive widths -> `HOLD_E43_RUNTIME_OR_INTEGRITY`;

No scientific parameter or threshold may be changed in the recovery implementation.
