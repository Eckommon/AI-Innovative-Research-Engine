# CLM-171 — PORTFOLIO-R27 selects US-MINE-F01

Under the outcome-blind R27 Mission-ROI comparison, **US-MINE-001 scores 39/45 and is selected for one Stage-0 US-MINE-F01 feasibility gate** after terminal US-BRIDGE-E01.

The selection is a portfolio-control judgment, not an empirical injury relationship. Durable state shows US-UTIL has already completed F01/F02, while US-MINE and US-PIPE remain unexecuted candidates and C-EU-004 remains a preserved Wave-1 join asset. R27 therefore evaluates current marginal information value rather than prior ranking alone.

Current official MSHA documentation supports a mine/time/subunit structural route between quarterly employment/production reporting and accident/injury records, but also states that Metal/Nonmetal operators are not required to report production. This unresolved reporting-semantic boundary is exactly why only an outcome-blind F01 is selected.

No injury magnitude, severity distribution, injury rate, production-per-hour magnitude, or exposure→injury relationship was opened for R27 selection.
