# DEC-120 — Select U.S. Aviation Weather–Delay Propagation for the Next Bounded Gate
# DEC-120 — 다음 제한 gate로 미국 항공 기상–지연 전파 병목 선정

**`SELECT_C_US_004_AVIATION_WEATHER_DELAY_PROPAGATION`**

Select:

**C-US-004 — U.S. Aviation Weather–Delay Propagation Intelligence**

**US-AIR-F01 — BTS On-Time Airport/Flight Identity × NOAA LCDv2 Weather-Station Join Feasibility**  
Issue #88.

## Rationale / 근거

The candidate offers:
- source-defined direct operational delay fields;
- stable and time-specific official airport identifiers;
- official BTS airport coordinates/time-zone attributes;
- current NOAA LCDv2 station metadata and bulk CSV weather access;
- a plausible large independent weather-station universe;
- high cross-agency and practical transportation-resilience value;
- a fresh branch with low immediate diminishing-return risk.

The decisive uncertainty is not whether there are many flight rows. It is whether a prospective official airport→NOAA station mapping yields enough **independent environmental exposure units** after nested flights and shared stations are handled correctly.

That uncertainty is scientific/source-semantic and can be resolved before any effect estimate.

## Frozen outcome qualification / 고정 outcome 자격검증

Prioritize:
**BTS `DepDelayMinutes`**.

Do not use `WeatherDelay` as the primary outcome because it is already an attributed cause-of-delay field and would create avoidable weather-label leakage.

## Frozen support boundary / 고정 support 경계

US-AIR-F01 PASS requires at least:
- 50 qualified origin AirportIDs;
- 40 unique NOAA station identities;
- complete calendar-year 2025 common support;
- deterministic spatial/time alignment;
- explicit anti-pseudoreplication exposure-key counts.

Flight rows nested under the same station-time exposure must not be counted as independent weather replications.
