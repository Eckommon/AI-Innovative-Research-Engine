# DEC-132 — Freeze US-UTIL-F02 2019–2024 outcome-blind panel-feasibility contract
# DEC-132 — US-UTIL-F02 2019–2024 결과 비사용 panel-feasibility 계약 고정

## Decision / 결정

Authorize Issue #96 `US-UTIL-F02` only under the prospectively frozen contract in `research/US-UTIL-F02/README.md`.

## Frozen scope / 고정 범위

- Years: **2019–2024 final EIA-861** only.
- NOAA Storm Events matched by year.
- Identity: exact EIA Utility Number only.
- Reliability: inspect identity and non-outcome reporting-method / major-event metadata only; do not parse SAIDI/SAIFI/CAIDI magnitudes.
- Advanced Metering: inspect schema/field routes only; do not parse AMI/AMR/standard-meter count magnitudes.
- Service Territory: preserve utility↔county many-to-many structure; no invented customer weights.
- Geography: deterministic county identities/crosswalk diagnostics; record drift/exclusions explicitly.

## Frozen PASS thresholds / 고정 PASS 기준

All must pass:
- >=500 unique utilities with Reliability + AMI + Service Territory support in at least 4 of 6 years;
- >=300 utilities with at least 4 years on one prospectively identifiable comparable Reliability reporting basis;
- >=2,000 qualified utility-year observations satisfying repeated support;
- >=8,000 qualified utility-year×county mappings;
- NOAA county-key route reproducible for each 2019–2024 year;
- future AMI numerator/denominator field route identifiable for every selected year without reading magnitudes.

Failure returns to Stage 0 without lowering thresholds or substituting outcome/predictor definitions.

## Promotion boundary / 승격 경계

Even PASS means **PANEL_DESIGN_READY / not RELATIONSHIP_TESTED**. A later E01 requires a new preregistration for one Reliability outcome/basis, exact AMI penetration definition, storm exposure aggregation/window, many-to-many dependence, confounding/baseline, falsification, materiality and novelty/utility plan.

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**.
