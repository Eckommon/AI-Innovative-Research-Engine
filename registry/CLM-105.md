# CLM-105 — E30 P2 and P3 belong to different geometry roles; the combined endpoint is mixed, not pure spatial robustness
# CLM-105 — E30 P2·P3는 서로 다른 geometry 역할이며 combined endpoint는 순수 공간 강건성이 아닌 혼합 endpoint이다

**KO:** Current NIST design mapping에 따르면 E30의 P2는 5 mm pad의 두 번째 cross-section이고 P3는 1 mm pad section이다. 따라서 기존 E30 수치에서 `Delta_P2=+24.287722773 µm`는 same-5-mm-geometry spatial sensitivity이고, `Delta_P3=+35.907503409 µm`는 alternate-1-mm-geometry directional contrast이다. 기존 equal-weight P2/P3 `Delta_combined=+30.097613091 µm`와 exact permutation `p=0.05`는 사전등록 공식에 따른 역사적 계산으로 보존되지만, geometry+position을 섞는 composite이므로 pure spatial-robustness 근거로 해석할 수 없다. 독립 표본수는 여전히 physical plate 기준 3 vs 3이다.

**EN:** Under the current authoritative NIST design mapping, E30 P2 is the second cross-section of the 5 mm pad while P3 is the 1 mm pad section. Accordingly, the historical E30 `Delta_P2=+24.287722773 µm` is a same-5-mm-geometry spatial sensitivity and `Delta_P3=+35.907503409 µm` is an alternate-1-mm-geometry directional contrast. The historical equal-weight P2/P3 `Delta_combined=+30.097613091 µm` and exact permutation `p=0.05` are preserved as computations under the preregistered formula, but the endpoint mixes geometry and position and cannot be interpreted as pure spatial robustness. Independent n remains three physical plates versus three physical plates.
