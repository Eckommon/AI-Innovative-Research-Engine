# CLM-088 — E24 sign reversal is structural and the fully adjusted positive slope is residual
# CLM-088 — E24 부호 반전은 구조적이며 완전조정 양의 slope는 residual이다

**Claim / 주장:** D25 reproduced standardized slopes of pooled `-0.278047`, part-adjusted `-1.026589`, block-adjusted `-0.022349`, and part+block-adjusted `+0.015305`. Frozen `STRUCTURAL_SIGN_REVERSAL=YES`, while `BLOCK_REMOVAL_EXPLAINS_REVERSAL=NO` because block adjustment alone did not change the sign.

All four part-specific x↔y Spearman diagnostics are negative. The positive E24 slope appears only after both part and block fixed effects are removed and remains materially small (`partial R²=0.019321`).

**Boundary / 경계:** This does not establish a positive causal local process effect; it identifies a small residual estimand after dominant hierarchical structure is removed.

**Evidence / 근거:** `research/AMBENCH-D25/EXECUTION_RESULT.md`, `research/AMBENCH-D25/RESULT.md`.
