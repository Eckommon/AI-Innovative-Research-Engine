# DEC-123 — Select US-AIR first relationship test after JOIN_READY
# DEC-123 — JOIN_READY 이후 US-AIR 최초 관계검증 선정

## Decision / 결정

PORTFOLIO-R09 selects **US-AIR-E01** as the next bounded mission gate. / PORTFOLIO-R09는 다음 제한 미션 gate로 US-AIR-E01을 선정한다.

Selection code:

**`SELECT_C_US_004_FIRST_RELATIONSHIP_TEST`**

## Rationale / 근거

US-AIR-F01 produced 263 final origin airports, 263 unique NOAA stations and 95,995 station×calendar-date source keys without opening weather or delay effects. That creates a high-information first relationship test with a direct operational outcome and substantially stronger prospective unit diversity than the immediately available AU-NEM, Singapore or post-HOLD Canada redesign alternatives. / 효과 비노출 상태의 대규모 join asset이 있어 첫 관계검증의 기대 정보가치가 가장 높다.

C-EU-001 remains high-value but is not selected for the immediate slot because previously recorded credential/operability friction remains unresolved by new project evidence. / EU grid는 고가치 후보로 보존한다.

## Boundary / 경계

This decision does not authorize immediate delay-effect calculation. E01 must first freeze one weather variable and pass an outcome-blind source-quality Stage A. / 본 결정은 즉시 지연효과 계산을 승인하지 않으며 E01은 먼저 단일 기상변수와 Stage A 품질 gate를 통과해야 한다.

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**.
