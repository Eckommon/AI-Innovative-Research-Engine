# CLM-048 — `mds2-2525` is a qualified external same-experiment validation candidate

**KO:** NIST `mds2-2525`는 BP4 IN718과 다른 재료·실험조건에서 integrating-sphere time-resolved absorptance와 high-speed X-ray melt-pool measurements를 동시에 취득한 공개 PDR 자산이며, Al challenge에는 time-dependent absorption과 physical geometry result components가 공개되어 있다. 따라서 D12 이후 외부 physical-validation 후보로 자격이 있다.

**EN:** NIST `mds2-2525` is a public PDR asset acquired under material and experimental contexts distinct from BP4 IN718, combining integrating-sphere time-resolved absorptance with simultaneous high-speed X-ray melt-pool measurements. The Al challenge exposes time-dependent absorption and physical-geometry result components, qualifying it as an external physical-validation candidate after D12.

**Boundary / 경계:** Qualification as a candidate does not establish repeat-resolved replication, numerical equivalence to BP4 coupling, or predictive/generalizable value.

**Evidence / 근거:** NIST A-AMB2022-01 challenge page; NIST PDR `mds2-2525` release/component metadata; Simonds et al. 2024 benchmark article.
