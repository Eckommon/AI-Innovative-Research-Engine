# DEC-110 — Select C-JP-001 Japan Port Weather–Throughput Stress
# DEC-110 — C-JP-001 일본 항만 기상–물동량 스트레스 선정

## Decision / 결정

After `PORTFOLIO-R05`, select:

**`C-JP-001 — Japan Port Weather–Throughput Stress`**

Authorize exactly one bounded next gate:

**`JP-PORT-F01 — MLIT Port-Month × JMA Weather-Station Deterministic Join Feasibility`**.

## Basis / 근거

- U.S.-grid F01 is preserved, but its immediate replicated cross-BA descendant is structurally too sparse.
- Japan offers a multi-port monthly outcome surface from an official Fundamental Statistics source.
- JMA provides official station-based historical weather with documented quality/homogeneity flags.
- Both source families are zero-price and can be source-semantically inspected without provisioning an API token.
- Multi-port × month units offer more independent-unit diversity than the single Singapore national-port monthly series.
- C-EU-001 remains equally high in scientific potential but currently requires ENTSO-E and CDS credential provisioning before full automated execution.

## F01 boundary / F01 경계

F01 may establish only source identity, port/station identity, spatial mapping, monthly/daily time semantics, quality flags, snapshot hashes and prospective support rules.

No port-weather outcome association may be computed.

## Cost / 비용

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**. Any potentially billable work requires explicit prior approval.
