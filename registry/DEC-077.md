## Why F37 / F37 사유

The project now contains multiple controlled evidence strands relevant to path-dependent thermal history:
1. AMBENCH-E29: independent physical-plate evidence in AMB2025-07 IN718 showing a strong directional overlap-depth difference between 0.75 ms and 5.0 ms turnaround conditions;
2. AMBENCH-E33: independent IN625 rapid-turnaround scan-strategy experiment showing a strong association between opposite prior-scan histories and melt-pool geometry under equivalent programmed track lengths;
3. AMBENCH-E36: independent NIST RHF IN625 parameter-sweep experiment showing lower part-level melt-pool-area variability for the non-selective RHF variable-power group versus constant-power baseline.

Before proposing a broader innovation/control primitive, F37 must audit whether these experiments genuinely support one shared mechanism class or only superficially similar directional observations.

## F37 boundaries / F37 경계

F37 is source/claim synthesis first, not a new high-capacity model. It must:
- preserve material, machine, geometry, measurand, intervention and exposure differences;
- distinguish turnaround-time effects, scan-order/history effects and RHF feedforward compensation;
- identify common causal/mechanistic assertions that are supported versus merely plausible;
- falsify any attempted cross-experiment equivalence that is not warranted;
- classify resulting innovation hypothesis strength and exact next external-validation requirement;
- use zero incremental monetary cost only.

No new Skill/MCP/Plugin promotion is justified solely by E36; workflow remains `SHARED-INTERNAL-CANDIDATE` pending demonstrated cross-project reuse.
