# CLM-133 — Current U.S. official aviation/weather sources expose a high-potential independent-unit join surface
# CLM-133 — 현행 미국 공식 항공/기상 source는 독립단위 검증 가치가 높은 join surface를 제공

## Claim / 주장

Current official source metadata establish that:

1. BTS Marketing Carrier On-Time Performance exposes stable/time-specific airport identities plus direct operational departure-delay, cancellation/diversion and local-time fields;
2. the BTS Aviation Master Coordinate support table exposes the corresponding airport identity, coordinates, UTC/local-time variation and time-valid attribute dates;
3. NOAA/NCEI LCDv2 is the current Local Climatological Data product, provides station metadata/search plus bulk CSV access, and contains hourly/daily weather observations from U.S. observing systems including ASOS/AWOS.

These source semantics justify promoting **C-US-004** to an outcome-blind F01 that tests deterministic airport→weather-station identity and true independent exposure-unit cardinality.

They do **not** yet establish:
- a PASS join;
- >=50 qualified airports;
- >=40 unique NOAA stations;
- weather completeness;
- a weather–delay effect.

Those are US-AIR-F01 questions.

## Primary official sources / 주요 공식 출처

- U.S. DOT / BTS TranStats — Marketing Carrier On-Time Performance (Beginning January 2018)
- U.S. DOT / BTS Aviation Support Tables — Master Coordinate
- NOAA / NCEI — Local Climatological Data Version 2

## Evidence boundary / 증거 경계

No weather values or delay magnitudes were analyzed to form this claim. The claim is source/schema qualification for portfolio promotion only.
