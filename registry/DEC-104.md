# DEC-104 — Select C-US-003R Critical Mineral Entry-Node Concentration Intelligence
# DEC-104 — C-US-003R 핵심광물 진입노드 집중도 지능화 선정

## Decision / 결정

After mandatory Stage 0 `PORTFOLIO-R03`, select:

**`C-US-003R — Critical Mineral Import-Source × U.S. Entry-Node Concentration Intelligence`**

as the next mission branch.

Authorize exactly one bounded next gate:

**`US-MINERAL-F01 — USGS Critical-Mineral Trade-Code × Census Import-Node Join Feasibility`**.

## Why selected / 선정 이유

The branch has the strongest current balance of:
- a direct strategic supply-chain bottleneck question;
- cross-agency public-data relationship value;
- a published official semantic bridge from mineral identities to trade codes;
- country-of-origin plus U.S. customs-district/transport-mode structure in Census trade products;
- an all-or-predefined-universe design that can be made falsifiable without commodity cherry-picking;
- practical intervention value around sourcing, gateway diversification, logistics, stockpiling, domestic processing and recycling;
- zero-cost public-file access without dashboard scraping or paid commercial trade data.

The selected research question is narrower and more operational than the original Wave 1 `C-US-003`: whether strategic mineral exposure can be decomposed into **foreign-source concentration and domestic entry-node concentration** using deterministic official identities.

## Current source basis / 현행 소스 근거

### USGS

- Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 is current and public, with more than 90 mineral/material chapters and a public 2021–2025 data release.
- MCS includes tariff information, import sources and net-import-reliance context.
- USGS methodology/technical input for the 2025 U.S. List of Critical Minerals publishes mineral-to-trade-code mappings, with weights/notes where needed.

### Census

- U.S. international-trade data products are currently public at no cost.
- 2026 merchandise-import products expose HTSUSA commodity code, country of origin, customs district of entry/unlading, method of transportation, quantity, shipping weight and value.
- Census API access now requires a key, so F01 shall prefer public file/bulk routes rather than create credential work for convenience.

## F01 authorization boundary / F01 승인 경계

F01 may verify only:
1. critical-mineral universe/version identity;
2. USGS mineral→HTS/HS code mappings, weights and caveats;
3. Census public-file schema for code, origin country, entry/unlading district, mode, quantity/value and time;
4. vintage/granularity compatibility;
5. duplicate/revision/suppression/unit semantics;
6. deterministic join keys and snapshot/hash rules;
7. whether a prospective concentration experiment can include the full or a prospectively support-qualified mineral universe.

F01 shall **not** calculate or rank:
- source-country concentration;
- customs-district/entry-node concentration;
- HHI or related concentration indices;
- mineral risk rankings;
- gateway risk rankings;
- correlations, regressions or policy recommendations.

## Branch-stop / 중단

HOLD/REJECT if the official mapping and Census public data require unsupported manual many-to-many assignment, mixed-product codes make the mineral unit non-identifiable, or the necessary import-node fields are unavailable through the zero-cost public-file route.

Do not rescue the branch with:
- paid commercial trade databases;
- scraping of visualization tools;
- arbitrary commodity-code substitutions;
- post-outcome commodity selection.

Held/dormant routes remain unchanged under prior decisions.
