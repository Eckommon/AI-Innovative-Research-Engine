# DEC-188 — Authorize US-UTIL-N01 AMI-ramp design

## Decision / 결정

Authorize Issue #137 / US-UTIL-N01 as an outcome-blind descendant-design gate using exactly one exposure family: annual change in exact utility-state EIA-861 AMI penetration, with the denominator fixed as AMI Total + AMR Total + Standard(non-AMR/AMI) Total.

Future Reliability basis is frozen to IEEE SAIDI With MED change from exposure year `t` to `t+1`, but N01 may inspect that basis only as blank/nonblank structural support. SAIDI/SAIFI/CAIDI magnitudes remain closed.

## Design rationale / 설계 근거

- F02 already established a reproducible 2019–2024 Reliability/Advanced-Metering panel route and adjudicated IEEE-with-MED comparability.
- Utility-state-year avoids invented national aggregation for multi-state utilities.
- Global single-use of Utility Number prevents one multi-state utility from creating pseudoreplicated independent evidence.
- Exact state/year matching plus prospective AMI-share and meter-size calipers create a deterministic non-causal comparison without using outcome values.
- No county storm weights are invented; storm-adjusted sensitivity requires later prospective authorization.

## Boundary / 경계

- Reliability magnitudes unopened in N01.
- No AMI→Reliability relationship computed in N01.
- No post-support denominator/threshold/caliper/year/state rescue.
- Future -30 minutes/customer-year materiality is a project-defined decision threshold, not an EIA/IEEE standard.
- Incremental monetary cost remains 0 USD.
