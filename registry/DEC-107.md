# DEC-107 — Select C-US-001 U.S. Grid Bottleneck Intelligence
# DEC-107 — C-US-001 미국 전력망 병목 지능화 선정

## Decision / 결정

After mandatory Stage 0 `PORTFOLIO-R04`, select:

**`C-US-001 — U.S. Grid Bottleneck Intelligence`**

as the next mission branch.

Authorize exactly one bounded next gate:

**`US-GRID-F01 — LBNL Queued Up Operator/Region × EIA-930 Balancing-Authority Identity & Bottleneck-Outcome Feasibility`**.

## Why selected / 선정 이유

The critical-mineral branch has now produced a validated preregistered result and therefore faces lower marginal information value for immediate same-branch variants. `DEC-106` prohibits automatic continuation through alternate year/threshold/geography or HS6-port extension.

`C-US-001` now has the strongest expected new scientific information value because:
- interconnection queues are a direct U.S. infrastructure bottleneck with project-level operational consequences;
- LBNL Queued Up 2026 provides a current, public, project-level queue dataset through end-2025 across all seven ISOs/RTOs plus 50 non-ISO utilities;
- EIA-930 provides current public balancing-authority operating data through a keyless bulk route;
- a successful identity bridge could support a genuinely cross-source controlled experiment rather than another descriptive variant of a completed branch;
- the remaining uncertainty is scientific/semantic — whether queue operator/region units and EIA balancing authorities form a defensible common unit and whether a direct queue bottleneck outcome can be frozen prospectively.

## F01 authorization boundary / F01 승인 경계

F01 may verify only:
1. Queued Up 2026 file/version/hash and official schema semantics;
2. queue project/operator/region/status/date fields and candidate direct bottleneck outcomes;
3. EIA-930 bulk route/version/hash and balancing-authority identity fields;
4. deterministic operator/region ↔ balancing-authority identity rules;
5. bounded common cohort/time coverage;
6. duplicate/null/revision/snapshot rules;
7. whether a future experiment can use the full qualified universe or a prospectively support-qualified subset.

F01 shall not compute or rank:
- operator/BA queue delays;
- EIA demand/generation/interchange stress;
- queue-vs-grid relationships;
- correlations/regressions;
- causal effects;
- operator/system/policy/investment rankings.

## Branch-stop / 중단

HOLD/REJECT rather than rescue if:
- queue operator/region and EIA balancing authority require unsupported manual many-to-many geography mapping;
- no direct project-level queue bottleneck outcome can be frozen reproducibly;
- a usable universe depends on choosing operators after observing outcomes;
- paid/commercial data, opaque visualization scraping or credential-heavy access would be required.

Do not substitute a different grid-stress proxy inside F01 merely because it maps more easily.
