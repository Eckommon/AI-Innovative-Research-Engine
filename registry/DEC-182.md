# DEC-182 — Select US-MINE-F01 after terminal US-BRIDGE-E01

Select **US-MINE-F01** as PORTFOLIO-R27's next independent Stage-0 branch at **39/45**.

Rationale:
- US-MINE-001 remains structurally unexecuted even though it was previously screened in DISCOVERY-R01;
- official MSHA data expose mine/time/subunit identities and separate employment/production and accident/injury records at zero incremental monetary cost;
- the immediate outcome-blind gate can resolve material sector, contractor and denominator/reporting semantics before injury values are used;
- US-UTIL has already consumed F01/F02 source and panel uncertainty and remains highly overlapped;
- US-PIPE retains direct cause/outcome overlap;
- C-EU-004 remains preserved but its current outcome framing is less direct operationally.

The selection is not an empirical mine-safety finding and does not validate novelty. MSHA explicitly notes that Metal/Nonmetal operators are not required to report production, so a national production-pressure exposure must not be assumed. F01 must resolve reporting comparability before any sector scope or exposure definition is promoted.

Do not rescue US-BRIDGE-E01. Do not open injury magnitudes or compute an exposure→injury relationship during R27 or US-MINE-F01.

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**.
