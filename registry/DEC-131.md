# DEC-131 — R12 selects US-UTIL-F02 longitudinal comparability preflight
# DEC-131 — R12에서 US-UTIL-F02 다년 비교가능성 사전검증 선정

## Decision / 결정

PORTFOLIO-R12 selects **US-UTIL-F02** as the next bounded mission gate.

Selection code:

**`SELECT_C_US_005_LONGITUDINAL_COMPARABILITY_PREFLIGHT`**

## Rationale / 근거

US-UTIL-F01 has already removed the 2024 join/identity bottleneck, but any AMI × storm × reliability relationship would still be vulnerable to cross-year reporting-method drift, major-event-day semantics, county-boundary/service-territory changes and AMI field comparability. EIA's official documentation confirms multi-year source availability but also permits Reliability reporting under IEEE or other methods. / 즉시 효과실험보다 다년 측정체계 비교가능성 검증의 정보가치가 높다.

Generic AMI resilience/outage-management benefits also have prior official and research precedent, so no novelty credit is granted merely for estimating an AMI coefficient. / 일반 AMI resilience 메커니즘은 신규성으로 간주하지 않는다.

C-US-006 remains the second-ranked fresh independent opportunity; its hydrology route is strong but its direct long historical lock-delay route remains less immediately operational than EIA's annual utility frame.

## Boundary / 경계

DEC-131 does **not** authorize reading SAIDI/SAIFI, AMI meter-count magnitudes, storm severity/damage values, or estimating any relationship. Only the F02 outcome-blind panel-feasibility gate is authorized.

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**.
