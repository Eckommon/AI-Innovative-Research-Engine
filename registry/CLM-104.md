# CLM-104 — AMB2025-07 alternate 1 mm pad geometry has a deterministic same-six-plate P3 route
# CLM-104 — AMB2025-07 대체 1 mm pad geometry는 동일 six-plate P3 결정론적 route를 가진다

**KO:** Current NIST `mds2-4103` v1.0.0의 checksum-verified `SampleIParameters.csv`와 공식 AMB2025-06/07 design documentation은 bare turnaround plate T72/T82/T92(0.75 ms) 및 T102/T112/T122(5.00 ms) 각각에서 P1/P2가 5 mm pad, P3가 1 mm pad임을 직접 식별한다. Current NERDm은 이 six plates의 P1/P2/P3 track-result component를 각각 unique path, size, SHA-256으로 식별한다. 따라서 1 mm alternate geometry는 별도 plate 집단이 아니라 동일 six physical plates의 P3로 결정론적으로 연결된다.

**EN:** The checksum-verified current NIST `mds2-4103` v1.0.0 `SampleIParameters.csv` and official AMB2025-06/07 design documentation directly identify P1/P2 as 5 mm pad sections and P3 as the 1 mm pad section for each bare turnaround plate T72/T82/T92 (0.75 ms) and T102/T112/T122 (5.00 ms). Current NERDm uniquely identifies the P1/P2/P3 track-result components for these six plates by path, size, and SHA-256. The 1 mm alternate geometry therefore maps deterministically to P3 on the same six physical plates rather than to a separate plate group.
