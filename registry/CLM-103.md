# CLM-103 — Independent-n boundary remains valid; P2/P3 spatial-repeat framing corrected
# CLM-103 — 독립 n 경계는 유효; P2/P3 공간 반복 해석은 보정

## Correction notice / 보정 공지
Current authoritative NIST design mapping establishes P2 as a 5 mm-pad cross-section and P3 as the 1 mm-pad cross-section. Therefore the prior sentence treating **both** P2 and P3 as same-geometry spatial repeated measurements is superseded by `CLM-105` and `DEC-065`.

## Preserved valid boundary / 보존되는 유효 경계

**KO:** E30의 P2/P3 observations는 E29와 동일한 six physical plates에서 얻어졌으므로 P2/P3 및 track rows를 독립 physical samples로 계산할 수 없다. Primary inferential n은 여전히 3 plates 대 3 plates이다. E30은 독립 build/plate replication 또는 광범위한 인과 일반화를 제공하지 않는다.

**EN:** E30 P2/P3 observations came from the same six physical plates as E29, so neither section observations nor track rows can be counted as independent physical samples. The inferential n remains three plates versus three plates. E30 does not provide independent build/plate replication or broad causal generalization.

## Corrected role split / 보정된 역할 구분
- P2 = nested same-5-mm-geometry spatial location within plate;
- P3 = nested alternate-1-mm-geometry outcome within the same plate;
- both remain non-independent relative to plate identity, but they are **not the same scientific geometry role**.
