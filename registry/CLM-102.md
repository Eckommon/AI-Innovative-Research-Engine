# CLM-102 — Historical E30 P2/P3 numerical result; spatial framing corrected by DEC-065
# CLM-102 — E30 P2/P3 역사적 수치 결과; 공간 해석은 DEC-065로 보정

## Correction notice / 보정 공지
The numerical values below remain verified historical outputs of the preregistered E30 computation. The former statement that both P2 and P3 are same-geometry spatial repeats is superseded: current authoritative NIST design mapping establishes **P2 = 5 mm pad** and **P3 = 1 mm pad**. See `CLM-105`, `research/AMBENCH-E30/SEMANTIC_CORRECTION.md`, and `DEC-065`.

## Preserved historical computation / 보존된 역사적 계산

**KO:** 사전등록된 E30에서 0.75 ms minus 5.0 ms overlap-depth contrast는 P2에서 `+24.287722773 µm`, P3에서 `+35.907503409 µm`였다. P2/P3를 plate 내부에서 동일가중한 historical endpoint의 `Delta_combined = +30.097613091 µm`, exact one-sided permutation `p=0.05`, combined plate rank-biserial `1.0`(9 wins, 0 losses, 0 ties)이었다. 12개 plate-position 모두에서 공통 valid한 44 tracks sensitivity도 `Delta_common_combined = +30.097613091 µm`였다.

**EN:** In preregistered E30, the 0.75 ms minus 5.0 ms overlap-depth contrast was `+24.287722773 µm` at P2 and `+35.907503409 µm` at P3. The historical equal-weight P2/P3 endpoint had `Delta_combined = +30.097613091 µm`, exact one-sided permutation `p=0.05`, and combined plate rank-biserial `1.0` (9 wins, 0 losses, 0 ties). The sensitivity restricted to the 44 tracks valid in all twelve plate-position cells had `Delta_common_combined = +30.097613091 µm`.

## Current interpretation / 현재 해석
- P2: same-5-mm-geometry second-location spatial sensitivity;
- P3: alternate-1-mm-geometry directional contrast;
- P2/P3 combined: mixed geometry+position composite, **not** a pure spatial-robustness endpoint.
