# CLM-042 — Coupling Temporal Structure Is Heterogeneous / coupling 시간구조의 이질성

**KO:** D11에서 8개 frozen temporal descriptor 중 `5/8`은 `REPEAT_INFORMATIVE`, `2/8`은 `CASE_DOMINATED`, `1/8`은 `MIXED_VARIATION`이었다. 그러나 901-point normalized waveform의 `WF_MEDIAN_WITHIN=0.0043387546`, `WF_HIGH_REPEAT_FRACTION=0.0`으로 direct waveform amplitude는 강하게 case-structured였다. 동시에 descriptor `PCA95_DIM=6`이었다. 따라서 BP4 coupling은 descriptor 종류에 따라 repeat 변동과 case 구조가 공존하는 이질적 정보구조를 가진다.

**EN:** In D11, `5/8` frozen temporal descriptors were `REPEAT_INFORMATIVE`, `2/8` were `CASE_DOMINATED`, and `1/8` was `MIXED_VARIATION`. Yet the 901-point normalized waveform had `WF_MEDIAN_WITHIN=0.0043387546` and `WF_HIGH_REPEAT_FRACTION=0.0`, showing strongly case-structured direct waveform amplitude, while descriptor `PCA95_DIM=6`. BP4 coupling therefore exhibits a heterogeneous information structure in which repeat variation and case structure coexist depending on representation.
