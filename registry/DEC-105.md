# DEC-105 — Close US-MINERAL-F01 as PASS and Freeze the Eight-Mineral Descendant Universe
# DEC-105 — US-MINERAL-F01 PASS 종결 및 후속 8개 광물 universe 고정

## Decision / 결정

1. Finalize `US-MINERAL-F01` as:

**`PASS_US_MINERAL_TRADE_IMPORT_NODE_JOIN_READY`**.

2. Freeze the only primary mineral universe eligible for the immediate outcome-bearing descendant as:

**Antimony, Barite, Beryllium, Palladium, Phosphate, Potash, Rhodium, Tellurium**.

3. Freeze the optional HS6 published port-of-unlading universe as:

**Antimony, Barite, Palladium, Phosphate, Potash, Rhodium**.

4. Authorize exactly one next action before trade-value exposure:

**preregister `US-MINERAL-E01` outcome-blind**.

No Census 2023 trade row may be opened until the E01 README/contract is committed.

## Why PASS / PASS 근거

F01 established all required source-semantic conditions:

- authoritative Final 2025 U.S. List of Critical Minerals frozen at 60;
- USGS OFR 2025-1047 Appendix-2 mapping/version/hash qualified;
- Census `IMP_DETL` public static route exposes 10-digit HTS, country, district of entry, district of unlading, year/month, quantity/value and mode-related fields;
- 2023 was frozen as the compatibility period because the USGS assessment used 2023 data unless otherwise noted;
- a deterministic official Census 2023 HTS10 concordance route exists;
- all 60 minerals were classified before concentration exposure;
- non-identifiable units were excluded prospectively rather than repaired after outcomes;
- all 12 2023 Merchandise ZIPs are reachable;
- duplicate, revision, suppression, null, quantity/unit and manifest/hash rules are frozen;
- no outcome concentration statistic was computed.

The existence of `R6` exclusions for Indium and Lithium does not force PARTIAL because they are removed before outcome exposure under the preregistered support-qualified-subset rule. The admitted eight-mineral subset has no unresolved 2023 mapping code.

## Primary descendant construct / 1차 후속 construct

The immediate descendant shall test **2023 import trade-value exposure concentration**, not physical mineral tonnage concentration.

Reason:
- mapped HTS codes span heterogeneous physical quantity units and product forms;
- Census disclosure can suppress quantity/weight;
- `gen_val_mo` is consistently available at the required identity grain and aligns with Port-HS6 `value_mo` as General Imports, Total Value.

Therefore the downstream claim must be worded as concentration of **published general-import trade value associated with the frozen mineral mapping**, unless a separate physical-content conversion is independently qualified and preregistered later.

## Geography priority / 지리 우선순위

The E01 preregistration must explicitly choose and justify the primary domestic-node definition before values are opened.

Source-semantic preference:
1. **district of unlading** for the more physical arrival/gateway interpretation;
2. district of entry as a preregistered secondary/sensitivity view;
3. HS6 port-of-unlading only for the six-mineral identity-preserving subset and with the Census disclosure limitation stated explicitly.

No geography may be selected after viewing which produces a more interesting concentration result.

## E01 preregistration requirements / E01 사전등록 요건

Before outcome access, freeze at minimum:
- exact eight-mineral mapping from `MAPPING_IDENTIFIABILITY.md`;
- 2023-01 through 2023-12 period;
- one snapshot manifest version across all 12 monthly files;
- `gen_val_mo` as primary weight;
- primary domestic-node geography;
- country concentration statistic;
- domestic-node concentration statistic;
- any dual-concentration/coupling statistic and thresholds;
- duplicate/null/suppression handling imported unchanged from `EXECUTION_CONTRACT.md`;
- structural/cardinality gate that executes before numerical values;
- PASS/PARTIAL/NO/HOLD gates;
- public-result raw-value minimization rule.

## Scientific boundary / 과학적 경계

A later positive E01 result may support a claim about **trade-value exposure structure** across foreign sources and published U.S. entry/unlading nodes.

It shall not, by itself, prove:
- physical tonnage dependence;
- causal supply disruption risk;
- true transaction-level port routing;
- inventory adequacy;
- domestic processing dependence;
- policy or investment superiority.

## Stop rule / 중단 규칙

If E01 cannot be fully preregistered without inspecting 2023 concentration values, stop before outcome access.

If the first structural/data-integrity stage reveals that the frozen eight-mineral mapping cannot be executed unchanged, use HOLD rather than substitute minerals, change vintage, repair codes manually, or narrow the universe after seeing outcomes.

After the one bounded E01 experiment, return to a mission-level review before same-branch tuning or additional concentration variants.

## Cost / 비용

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**. Any potentially billable work requires explicit prior user approval.
