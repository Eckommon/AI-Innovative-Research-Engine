# CLM-046 — D12 Five Primary Descriptors Are Sampling-Robust but Condition-Specific / D12 5개 primary descriptor는 sampling-robust·condition-specific

**KO:** D11의 5개 `REPEAT_INFORMATIVE` descriptor(`iqr_mid`, `early_contrast`, `late_contrast`, `early_shape_slope`, `late_shape_slope`)는 D12의 factor-2/4 sampling-phase perturbation 대비 모두 `R_sampling >= 3`으로 `ROBUST_TO_SAMPLING_PHASE`였다. 동시에 5/5 모두 balanced case+repeat-index decomposition에서 `repeat_index_share <= 0.25`로 `CASE_SPECIFIC_RESIDUAL_DOMINANT`였다. 즉 frozen D12 규칙에서 해당 변동은 단순 sampling-phase 민감도나 공통 repeat-number 효과로 설명되지 않는다.

**EN:** All five D11 `REPEAT_INFORMATIVE` descriptors (`iqr_mid`, `early_contrast`, `late_contrast`, `early_shape_slope`, `late_shape_slope`) were `ROBUST_TO_SAMPLING_PHASE` in D12 with `R_sampling >= 3` under the factor-2/4 sampling-phase perturbations. At the same time, all `5/5` were `CASE_SPECIFIC_RESIDUAL_DOMINANT` in the balanced case+repeat-index decomposition with `repeat_index_share <= 0.25`. Under the frozen D12 rules, these variations are therefore not explained by simple sampling-phase sensitivity or a common repeat-number effect.
