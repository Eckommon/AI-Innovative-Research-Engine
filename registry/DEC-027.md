# DEC-027 — Finalize D11 as MIXED_TEMPORAL_INFORMATION / D11 혼합 시간정보 확정

## Decision / 결정

**KO:** `AMBENCH-D11`을 **`MIXED_TEMPORAL_INFORMATION`**으로 종료한다. direct normalized waveform은 강하게 process-case structured이지만, 8개 frozen derived temporal descriptor 중 5개는 substantial within-case repeat variation을 보이며 descriptor `PCA95_DIM=6`이다. 이 혼합구조를 사후 feature tuning으로 제거하거나 `repeat-level physical signal`로 승격하지 않는다.

**EN:** Finalize `AMBENCH-D11` as **`MIXED_TEMPORAL_INFORMATION`**. The direct normalized waveform is strongly process-case structured, while five of eight frozen derived temporal descriptors show substantial within-case repeat variation and descriptor `PCA95_DIM=6`. Do not tune away this mixed structure post hoc or promote it to a `repeat-level physical signal`.

## Rationale / 근거

- `CASE_DOMINATED_COUNT = 2/8`;
- `REPEAT_INFORMATIVE_COUNT = 5/8`;
- normalized waveform `WF_MEDIAN_WITHIN = 0.0043387546`;
- normalized waveform `WF_HIGH_REPEAT_FRACTION = 0.0`;
- descriptor `PCA95_DIM = 6`;
- no integrity failures; Run `32553063163` success.

The frozen case-proxy gate fails because only `2/8` descriptors are case-dominated and PCA95 dimension is above 3. The frozen repeat-level-information gate fails because the direct waveform does not meet either repeat-variation criterion. / 두 양극 gate 모두 완전 충족하지 않아 MIXED가 정확한 판정이다.

## Consequence / 후속

- no physical-outcome utility, prediction, generalization, or causality claim;
- no model-capacity escalation or FFT/wavelet/neural rescue on the same 21 tracks inside D11;
- next research, if continued, must be separately preregistered and should distinguish whether repeat-sensitive dispersion/edge/slope descriptors reflect reproducible temporal morphology, measurement/noise structure, or condition-specific instability;
- broader promotion still requires independent conditions or a qualified same-specimen physical outcome.
