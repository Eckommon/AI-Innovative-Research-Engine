# CLM-038 — Same-BP4 Confocal Measurement Verified / 동일 BP4 confocal 측정 사실 검증

**KO:** NIST 공식 AMB2022-03 Version 1.01은 `AMB2022-718-SH1-BP4`를 dynamic laser coupling single-track plate로 명시하고, BP4를 포함한 laser-coupling specimen들이 ex-situ laser scanning confocal microscopy로 측정되어 complete 3D surface profile을 취득했다고 기록한다. 따라서 동일 BP4에서 coupling과 distinct ex-situ 3D topography 측정이 모두 수행됐다는 measurement-level provenance는 검증된다.

**EN:** The official NIST AMB2022-03 Version 1.01 identifies `AMB2022-718-SH1-BP4` as the dynamic-laser-coupling single-track plate and states that the laser-coupling specimens, including BP4, were measured ex situ by laser scanning confocal microscopy to obtain complete 3D surface profiles. Measurement-level provenance therefore verifies that both coupling and a distinct ex-situ 3D-topography measurement were performed on the same BP4 specimen.

**Evidence:** NIST AMB2022-03 Version 1.01; `research/AMBENCH-F10/RESULT.md`.
