# DEC-117 — Close CA-RAIL-E01 as Source-Integrity HOLD and Return to Stage 0
# DEC-117 — CA-RAIL-E01을 source-integrity HOLD로 종결하고 Stage 0로 복귀

## Decision / 결정

Finalize CA-RAIL-E01 as:

**HOLD_CA_RAIL_E01_WEATHER_SOURCE_INTEGRITY**

Stage B is not authorized.

## Why / 이유

The exact preregistered weather source contract failed before rail-dwell relationship exposure:
- only 77.755102% of possible station-weeks meet 7/7 Min Temp completeness;
- Prince Rupert and Lethbridge have zero numeric Min Temp support under the frozen station identities;
- Prince George has only 86 qualified weeks;
- the frozen gate requires >=90% overall and >=90 weeks for every station.

## Prohibited rescue / 금지된 rescue

Do not inside this E01:
- switch to another weather variable;
- choose a different nearby ECCC station;
- relax 7/7 completeness;
- remove low-support terminals/stations;
- change the reporting window;
- inspect rail-dwell outcomes to guide redesign.

Those are new hypotheses/designs.

## Mandatory next action / 의무 다음 행동

Return to **Stage 0 portfolio review**.

A future Canada-rail continuation may be considered only as a new candidate competing against preserved alternatives. It must receive a redesign/diminishing-return penalty because the first preregistered descendant could not reach Stage B.

## Cost / 비용

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**. Any potentially billable action requires explicit prior approval.
