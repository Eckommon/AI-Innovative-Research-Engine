# CLM-129 — Monthly extreme wind is negatively associated with Japanese port cargo in the preregistered panel
# CLM-129 — 사전등록 일본 항만 패널에서 월별 극대풍속은 항만 화물과 음의 연관성을 보임

## Claim / 주장

**KO:** 2019–2024의 사전 고정 10,165개 항만-월 관측치(143개 항만, 126개 JMA station)를 사용하고, JMA 품질코드 8의 일 최대풍속을 월별 최대값으로 집계한 뒤 항만 고정효과와 연-월 고정효과를 통제한 사전등록 패널 회귀에서, 극대풍속은 월별 총 해상출입화물의 `log1p`와 통계적으로 검출 가능한 음의 연관성을 보였다. 추정치 `beta=-0.006292975` per +1 m/s, JMA-station CR1 SE `0.002194361`, 95% CI `[-0.010635888,-0.001950062]`, two-sided `p=0.004853`. 사전 고정된 +5 m/s 기술적 변환은 약 **-3.10%**이다.

**EN:** In the preregistered 2019–2024 panel of 10,165 port-month observations (143 ports, 126 JMA stations), using the monthly maximum of quality-code-8 JMA daily maximum wind speed and controlling for port fixed effects plus year-month fixed effects, monthly extreme wind is detectably negatively associated with `log1p` monthly total maritime cargo. The frozen estimate is `beta=-0.006292975` per +1 m/s, JMA-station CR1 SE `0.002194361`, 95% CI `[-0.010635888,-0.001950062]`, two-sided `p=0.004853`. The preregistered descriptive +5 m/s translation is approximately **-3.10%**.

## Boundary / 경계

This is an **association claim**, not a causal claim.

It does not establish:
- that wind caused the observed cargo change;
- terminal-level shutdown or operational-loss mechanisms;
- physical mass tonnage loss;
- port-specific resilience/sensitivity rankings;
- optimal wind thresholds;
- lag/lead effects;
- policy or investment superiority.

The +5 m/s translation is a descriptive model implication only, not a causal effect or materiality threshold.
