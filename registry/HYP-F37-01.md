# HYP-F37-01 — Multi-Actuator Recent-Scan-History Control
# HYP-F37-01 — 다중 Actuator Recent-Scan-History 제어

## Hypothesis / 가설

A shared recent-scan-history state estimator may be used to coordinate multiple laser-processing actuators — laser power, turnaround/skywriting time, and local scan order/path selection — to stabilize melt-pool geometry against path-dependent heat accumulation.

Conceptual chain:
`recent scan events → history state H(t,x,y) → actuator policy {power, turnaround, local path/order} → melt-pool stability objective`.

## Evidence basis / 근거
- E29: turnaround timing is associated with a strong physical overlap-depth difference under a frozen 3-vs-3 physical-plate experiment;
- E33: equivalent programmed-length tracks exhibit a strong monotonic association between opposite prior-scan histories and melt-pool width/area;
- E36: residual-history-informed variable-power conditions show lower non-selective part-level melt-pool-area variability than constant-power baseline.

## Novelty warning / novelty 경고

`NOVELTY_UNVERIFIED` is mandatory.

RHF/history-aware power control is known prior art. This hypothesis does not claim novelty in RHF, feedforward power adjustment, thermal-history modeling, skywriting, or scan-order optimization separately.

Any potentially differentiating element would have to lie in a specific **shared state estimator + coordinated multi-actuator policy** and must survive a separate prior-art/novelty separation audit before prototype, patent or commercialization language is allowed.
