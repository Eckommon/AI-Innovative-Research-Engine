# DEC-028 — Monetary Cost Requires Prior User Approval / 금전비용은 사용자 사전승인 필수

## Decision / 결정

**KO:** `COST-001`의 승인 시점을 명확히 한다. 금전비용이 발생하거나 발생할 합리적 가능성이 있는 행동은 **비용 발생 전에** 사용자의 명시승인을 받아야 한다. 먼저 비용을 사용한 뒤 보고하거나 승인을 요청하는 방식은 허용되지 않으며, 사후 보고·사후 동의는 소급 승인으로 취급하지 않는다. 사전승인이 없거나 비용상태가 불명확하면 `HOLD_COST_APPROVAL`로 두고 실행하지 않는다.

**EN:** Clarify the timing requirement of `COST-001`. Any action that incurs, or has a reasonable possibility of incurring, monetary cost requires the user's explicit approval **before the cost-incurring action**. Spending first and reporting/requesting approval afterward is prohibited; after-the-fact reporting or consent is not retroactive authorization. If prior approval is absent or billing status is uncertain, classify the action as `HOLD_COST_APPROVAL` and do not execute.

## Consequence / 후속

- Zero-incremental-cost actions remain allowed without an extra approval when zero charge is established.
- Potentially billable API/cloud/GPU/CI/storage/data/service actions must stop before execution until a user approval record exists.
- Any future approved paid exception must record the approval before execution, scope, expected/maximum cost when knowable, and the research object requiring it.
