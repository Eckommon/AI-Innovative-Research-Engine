# CLM-120 — Current NESO Constraint and Historic-Demand Resources Support a Reproducible Daily Join
# CLM-120 — 현행 NESO 계통제약·과거수요 resource는 재현 가능한 일별 조인을 지원함

## Claim / 주장

A deterministic public-source route exists to align the selected current NESO Constraint Breakdown FY2026-27 resource with Historic Demand Data 2026 at a **one-calendar/settlement-date independent unit**, while preserving 46/48/50-period settlement-day semantics.

선정된 현행 NESO Constraint Breakdown FY2026-27와 Historic Demand Data 2026은 46/48/50 settlement-period 일 구조를 보존하면서 **1 calendar/settlement date 독립단위**로 결정론적 정렬이 가능하다.

The outcome-blind F01 preflight qualified 135 overlapping dates from `2026-04-01` through `2026-08-13` without requesting selected FY2026-27 numerical constraint costs/volumes or numerical demand/renewables/interconnector values.

## Boundary / 경계

This claim is limited to **source/schema/date-cardinality alignment feasibility**.

It does not establish association, prediction, causality, threshold behavior, or the superiority of any national system-state predictor for thermal, voltage or inertia constraints.

Because NESO may retrospectively refresh both constraint categorization and Historic Demand values, any later numerical claim requires a frozen extraction timestamp, exact resource identifiers/hashes, evaluation window and maturity rule.
