# CLM-190 — EPA cross-media E01 did not establish the preregistered positive relationship

Under the frozen 307-pair design and calendar-2024 E90 endpoint, Run `35168068999` observed HIGH **5.54%** vs LOW **3.58%**, RD **+1.95 percentage points**, `b/c=12/6`, and exact two-sided p **0.237885**. The point estimate is positive but below the +5pp materiality floor and statistically non-significant. This is a bounded observational result, not proof of no association generally and not a causal claim.
