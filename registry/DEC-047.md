# DEC-047 — Reject X16 histogram-only structural endpoint / X16 histogram 단독 구조 endpoint 거부

**Decision / 결정:** `AMBENCH-F21` final gate = **`REJECT_F21_ENDPOINT_ROUTE`**.

**KO:** F21은 workbook A/B 의미론과 XCT measurement context를 성공적으로 확립했지만, 그 결과 histogram workbook 단독으로 comparable structural-quality endpoint를 정의할 수 없다고 판정한다. 공통 threshold pore fraction은 금지하며, threshold-free centroid/IQR도 crop/background 및 XCT/EDM artifact 영향을 받는 imaging-distribution statistic이므로 physical defect quality로 재해석하지 않는다. X16 XCT dataset 자체와 F20 source-integrity PASS, F19 segmentation rule은 유지한다.

**EN:** F21 successfully qualifies the workbook A/B semantics and XCT measurement context, but concludes that the histogram workbook alone cannot support a comparable structural-quality endpoint. A common-threshold pore fraction is prohibited, and threshold-free centroid/IQR statistics are not silently reinterpreted as physical defect quality because they remain sensitive to crop/background composition and XCT/EDM artifacts. The X16 XCT dataset itself, F20 source-integrity PASS, and the frozen F19 segmentation rule remain valid.
