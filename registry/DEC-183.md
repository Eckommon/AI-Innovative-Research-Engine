# DEC-183 — Authorize US-MINE-F01 outcome-blind structural feasibility

Authorize Issue #134 / **US-MINE-F01** for source, schema, identity, time and exact structural join-support feasibility only.

The frozen 2019–2025 support window, exact `MINE_ID + CAL_YR + CAL_QTR + SUBUNIT_CD` identity, operator/contractor split and PASS/PARTIAL/HOLD thresholds in `research/US-MINE-F01/README.md` must be applied without rescue.

MSHA's official reporting semantics state that Metal/Nonmetal operators are not required to report production. F01 therefore cannot validate a national Coal+MNM production-pressure exposure. A PASS may establish structural join readiness only with `PRODUCTION_SCOPE_RESTRICTED` preserved.

F01 must not access or use injury degree, days lost/restricted, injury-count magnitude, severity, injury rate or any exposure-linked injury statistic. It must not compute production-per-hour. Structural record/key counts and blank/nonblank support are permitted solely for source/cardinality/identifiability gating.

Any later exposure choice or injury outcome access requires a separate outcome-blind N01 authorization after F01 terminalization.

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**.
