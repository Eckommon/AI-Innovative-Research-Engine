## Decision / 결정

Finalize F38 as **`NOVELTY_PARTIAL_GAP_F38`**.

Therefore `HYP-F37-01` remains only a narrow research-gap candidate with high adjacent-prior-art / obviousness risk.

## Exact next action / 정확한 다음 행동

**AMBENCH-F39 — Multi-Actuator Added-Value Falsification Design Gate.**

Before prototype expenditure or stronger novelty language, define a zero-cost falsification experiment in which the three-actuator joint policy must outperform strong known baselines:
1. constant parameters;
2. history-informed power-only;
3. history-informed power + timing/dwell;
4. thermal-history-informed path/order-only;
5. joint power + timing + path/order.

The comparison must use one frozen history-state definition, matched productivity/energy constraints, and physical melt-pool/thermal stability endpoints. Incremental value over the strongest comparator—not merely over constant parameters—is required.
