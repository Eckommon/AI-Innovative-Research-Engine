# CLM-076 — Histogram workbook alone is insufficient as a structural-quality endpoint / histogram workbook 단독 구조품질 endpoint 부적합

**KO:** X16 histogram workbook은 spatial information을 제거한 cropped XCT volume의 grayscale distribution이다. NIST는 part-specific threshold 필요성, Part 1-1의 larger crop, beam-hardening/residual-contrast artifacts, EDM-derived surface features를 문서화한다. 따라서 workbook 단독으로 common-threshold pore fraction이나 직접적인 structural-defect quality를 구성하면 exterior empty voxels, crop/background fraction 및 measurement/post-process artifacts를 구조 결함으로 혼동할 수 있다.

**EN:** The X16 histogram workbook is a spatially collapsed grayscale distribution of cropped XCT volumes. NIST documents the need for part-specific thresholds, a larger crop for Part 1-1, beam-hardening/residual-contrast artifacts, and EDM-derived surface features. Consequently, the workbook alone cannot support a common-threshold pore fraction or an unambiguous structural-defect quality endpoint without risking confounding by exterior empty voxels, crop/background fraction, and measurement/post-process artifacts.
