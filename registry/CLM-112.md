# CLM-112 — Bounded Path-Dependent Thermal-History Mechanism-Class Convergence
# CLM-112 — 제한된 Path-Dependent Thermal-History Mechanism-Class 수렴

## Claim / 주장

Three distinct NIST experiments provide bounded, directionally coherent evidence for a common **path-dependent recent-scan thermal-history mechanism class** across three different levers:
- turnaround timing (E29),
- scan order / accumulated prior-track history (E33),
- residual-history-informed laser-power control (E36).

The F37 frozen audit passes experiment independence, mechanism relevance, intervention triangulation, directional coherence, measurand triangulation and exposure/verification integrity. Material/context breadth and causal isolation remain PARTIAL.

## Boundary / 경계

This is **not**:
- a pooled common effect size;
- same-construct replication;
- proof of one uniquely isolated causal mechanism;
- universal cross-machine/material transfer;
- a novelty or patentability claim.

The three experiments differ in intervention, measurand, design and exposure history and must remain separate evidence strands.
