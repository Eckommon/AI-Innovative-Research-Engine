# DEC-178 — Authorize US-BRIDGE-N01 outcome-blind matched-interval design gate

Authorize Issue #131 / **US-BRIDGE-N01** exactly under `research/US-BRIDGE-N01/README.md`.

Authorized scope:
- FHWA NBI 2015–2025 legacy bridge/county/inspection identity reconstruction;
- non-outcome Items 27, 43B, 90, 91 and 106 for age/class/cadence/reconstruction design metadata;
- exact FEMA county/time identity restricted prospectively to `DR` Major Disaster Declarations and the already frozen physical-hazard incident set;
- consecutive 180–1095-day inspection intervals;
- outcome-blind exposed/control classification;
- deterministic state × bridge-class × pre-inspection-year 1:1 matching without replacement;
- exact matched-pair manifest and fingerprint;
- zero-cost derived counts/hashes/diagnostics with transient raw bytes.

Not authorized:
- slicing, parsing, decoding, persisting, summarizing, ranking, comparing or testing NBI condition Items 58/59/60/62;
- disaster-linked bridge-condition rates, changes or associations;
- fuzzy identity repair;
- changing frozen sources, windows, matching rules, thresholds or future E01 outcome/materiality rules after N01 execution.

If N01 passes, bridge condition values still remain closed until a separate US-BRIDGE-E01 authorization. Any later E01 must use the exact N01 matched-pair identity manifest and the prospectively frozen outcome/test rules.

Incremental monetary cost remains **0 USD**.
