# CLM-084 — Correct registration is stronger than the frozen +25-layer shift control, but both effects are small / 정합 registration이 +25-layer shift보다 강하지만 둘 다 작음

**KO:** AMBENCH-E24에서 primary registered association은 `beta=0.015305`, partial R² `0.019321`이고, 사전고정 +25-layer misregistration control은 `beta=0.011634`, partial R² `0.009379`였다. `|beta_primary| > |beta_shift25|` locality criterion은 PASS했지만 양쪽 effect 모두 작아 materiality PASS를 지원하지 않는다.

**EN:** In AMBENCH-E24, the primary registered association had `beta=0.015305` and partial R² `0.019321`, while the preregistered +25-layer misregistration control had `beta=0.011634` and partial R² `0.009379`. The locality criterion `|beta_primary| > |beta_shift25|` passed, but both effects are small and do not support the materiality PASS gate.
