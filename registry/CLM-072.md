# CLM-072 — X16 workbook deterministic sixteen-part sheet mapping / X16 workbook 16-part sheet mapping

**KO:** Immutable-verified `OverhangX16_ImageHistograms.xlsx`의 schema-only XML inspection은 `Plots` sheet와 정확히 16개의 `Part1_1`…`Part4_4` sheet를 확인했다. 모든 part sheet는 `A1:B256`, formula 0개였다. Numerical cell values는 출력·요약되지 않았다. 이 결과는 workbook-level part identity mapping을 확립하지만 columns A/B의 물리적 의미를 확립하지는 않는다.

**EN:** Schema-only XML inspection of the immutable-verified `OverhangX16_ImageHistograms.xlsx` established one `Plots` sheet and exactly sixteen `Part1_1`…`Part4_4` sheets. Every part sheet has dimension `A1:B256` and zero formulas. Numerical cell values were not emitted or summarized. This establishes workbook-level part identity mapping but not the physical semantics of columns A/B.
