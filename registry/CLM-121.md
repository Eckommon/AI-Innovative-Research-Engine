# CLM-121 — NESO Boundary Flow/Limit and Thermal-Cost Sources Share Exact Reproducible Constraint-Group Identity
# CLM-121 — NESO Boundary Flow/Limit와 Thermal-Cost source는 정확하고 재현 가능한 Constraint-Group identity를 공유함

## Claim / 주장

Current official NESO `Day Ahead Constraint Flows and Limits` and `Thermal Constraint Costs Data 26-27` share exact source-defined `Constraint Group` identifiers sufficient for a deterministic same-boundary temporal join without fuzzy or inferred boundary mapping.

현행 공식 NESO `Day Ahead Constraint Flows and Limits`와 `Thermal Constraint Costs Data 26-27`는 fuzzy 또는 추정 boundary mapping 없이 결정론적 동일-boundary 시간 조인에 사용할 수 있는 정확한 source-defined `Constraint Group` 식별자를 공유한다.

F02 found six exact common identifiers:
`ESTEX, SCOTEX, SEIMP, SSE-SP, SSHARN, SWALEX`.

Of these, `ESTEX` and `SCOTEX` have exact overlapping 2026 source coverage from `2026-04-01` through `2026-08-18`.

## Boundary / 경계

This claim is limited to **source identity and temporal-join feasibility**.

It does not establish numerical association, predictive value, causality, or that the day-ahead `Limit (MW)` field is exclusively a thermal engineering limit under every operating condition.

No observation-level `Limit`, `Flow` or `Daily Cost` value was requested or emitted in F02.
