## Why no automatic E01 / E01 자동진행 금지 이유

The join is technically strong, but the broad official region-weather route yields only four unique region-pair exposure units for six interconnectors.

Launching an immediate panel experiment and clustering as if all six interconnectors had independent weather exposure would be pseudoreplication.

A later AU-NEM descendant may compete again only if it prospectively provides:
- a defensible corridor-level exposure geometry; or
- an inference design valid with the small number of independent region-pair weather units.

## Mandatory next action / 의무 다음 행동

Return to Stage 0 portfolio review.

Preserve the AU-NEM source bridge as a reusable capability rather than forcing a low-independent-unit regression.
