# CLM-131 — The preregistered Canadian rail minimum-temperature panel fails its frozen weather-completeness gate
# CLM-131 — 사전등록 캐나다 철도 최저기온 패널은 고정 weather completeness gate를 충족하지 못함

## Claim / 주장

Under the frozen CA-RAIL-E01 source contract, only **1,143 of 1,470 possible ECCC station-weeks (77.755102%)** satisfy the preregistered 7/7 daily Minimum Temperature completeness rule.

The frozen requirement was >=90% overall and >=90 qualified weeks at every station.

Prince Rupert and Lethbridge provide zero numeric Min Temp days in both official 2024 and 2025 daily files under the frozen station identities, and Prince George provides only 86 qualified reporting weeks.

Therefore the preregistered experiment is not source-complete enough to proceed to its rail-dwell relationship stage without prohibited redesign.

## Boundary / 경계

This is a source-integrity claim, not a substantive claim that extreme cold has no rail effect.

No rail dwell magnitude or rail-weather coefficient was opened/calculated.
