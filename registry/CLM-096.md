# CLM-096 — NIST documents a deterministic pixel-to-geometry reconstruction contract / NIST는 결정론적 pixel-to-geometry reconstruction contract를 문서화한다

**KO:** NIST `mds2-4103` README는 `*_pixel_points.csv`가 측정과 계산에 사용된 pixel locations임을 명시하고, depth/width/bead-height/overlap-depth 좌표의 의미 및 계산 관계를 설명한다. Cross-sectional TIFF에는 physical micrometer-per-pixel scaling이 존재하며, overlap depth는 overlap-depth Y coordinate와 substrate-surface Y coordinate의 차이를 기반으로 정의된다.

**EN:** The NIST `mds2-4103` README states that `*_pixel_points.csv` contains pixel locations used for measurements and calculations, and documents the coordinate meanings and calculation relationships for depth, width, bead height, and overlap depth. Cross-sectional TIFFs have a physical micrometer-per-pixel scale, and overlap depth is defined from the difference between the overlap-depth Y coordinate and substrate-surface Y coordinate.
