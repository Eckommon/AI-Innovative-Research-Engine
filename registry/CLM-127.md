# CLM-127 — The immediate BA-year cross-BA E01 design is structurally too sparse
# CLM-127 — 즉시 BA-year cross-BA E01 설계는 구조적으로 표본 지지가 부족함

## Claim / 주장

**KO:** 결과값을 열지 않은 상태에서 2021–2023 completed-project 구조에 `BA-year >=3 completed projects`와 `BA >=2 qualifying years`의 사전 anti-pseudoreplication 규칙을 적용하면, threshold 이전에는 7개 BA-year cell이 존재하지만 최종적으로는 **ERCO 하나의 BA에서 2021/2022/2023 3개 cell만** 남는다(총 91 project). 따라서 현재 구조로는 BA 고정 차이를 분리하면서 여러 BA에서 반복되는 cross-BA EIA-930→IR→COD 관계를 검정하기에 충분한 independent support가 없다.

**EN:** Without opening queue-duration magnitudes or EIA operating values, applying the prospective anti-pseudoreplication rules `>=3 completed projects per BA-year` and `>=2 qualifying years per BA` to the 2021–2023 completed-project structure leaves only **three cells, all from ERCO (2021/2022/2023), totaling 91 projects**. The immediate design therefore lacks replicated cross-BA independent support for a within-BA EIA-930→IR→COD relationship test.

## Boundary / 경계

This does **not** invalidate `CLM-126` or the F01 PASS. The deterministic LBNL→EIA BA join remains a reusable asset.

A one-BA ERCO experiment could technically be designed, but it would no longer provide the intended replicated cross-BA evidence and is not automatically justified by this branch.
