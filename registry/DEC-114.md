# DEC-114 — Select C-CA-001 Canadian Freight Rail Weather–Delay Intelligence
# DEC-114 — C-CA-001 캐나다 화물철도 기상–지연 병목 지능화 선정

## Decision / 결정

Finalize WAVE3-GEO-D01 by selecting:

**C-CA-001 — Canadian Freight Rail Weather–Delay Intelligence**

Authorize exactly one bounded next gate:

**CA-RAIL-F01 — Transport Canada Weekly Terminal-Dwell × ECCC Weather-Station Identity & Join Feasibility**

## Why selected / 선정 이유

C-CA-001 provides:
- a direct weekly operational rail bottleneck outcome;
- a new national/domain branch;
- carrier-specific terminal-area identities;
- official public full-dataset access;
- official station/location/weather sources;
- high practical relevance to corridor resilience and freight reliability;
- a source-semantic spatial-temporal uncertainty that can be tested outcome-blind.

Australia NEM remains high value but carries more current report/version and constraint-to-weather mapping complexity and less portfolio diversification after prior grid branches.

## Frozen D01 outcome family / D01 고정 outcome family

The next F01 shall first qualify:

**Average Terminal Dwell Time - Loaded Cars and Intermodal Containers**, unit Hours.

Do not replace it with another rail performance measure because a later weather relation is stronger.

## Boundary / 경계

D01 discovered and selected a candidate only. It did not calculate a rail-weather relationship.

Any numerical weather/dwell experiment requires a separately preregistered descendant after F01 PASS.
