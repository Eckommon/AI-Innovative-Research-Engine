# DEC-097 — Finalize EU-ISR-F01 PASS; Do Not Force an Under-Specified Climate Association Experiment
# DEC-097 — EU-ISR-F01 PASS 종결 및 불충분한 기후 연관 실험 강제 금지

## Decision / 결정

Finalize `EU-ISR-F01` as:

**`PASS_EU_ISR_FACILITY_CLIMATE_JOIN_READY`**.

Preserve `C-EU-004 Industrial Site Climate Risk` as a validated cross-agency join asset, but do **not** automatically launch a facility climate-risk score or climate→emissions/energy regression.

Return to Stage 0 portfolio review.

## Why / 이유

F01 established a strong technical/scientific prerequisite: official EEA facility coordinates can be deterministically linked to NASA POWER meteorological time series without credential provisioning.

However, feasibility is not a hypothesis. Current EEA LCP outcomes such as energy input and pollutant emissions are jointly structured by facility capacity, fuel, dispatch, technology, regulation and country/system conditions. A simple cross-sectional climate association would therefore risk producing a statistically neat but scientifically ambiguous result.

`MEM-054` requires mission-level information value to outrank branch completion. The project should not manufacture an experiment merely because a join is available.
